# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant].

In short: be respectful and considerate, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussions about the work rather than the person. Harassment, insults and discriminatory
language are not acceptable in the issues, pull requests, discussions or any other space of
this project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that goes against this code, contact the maintainer
privately through the email address shown on his [GitHub profile][profile]. Reports are
handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions
that do not respect this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for
behaviour he judges inappropriate, threatening or harmful. The enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant apply.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
